⚡ Master Guide: Slopes & Areas

📐

What do GRAPH SLOPES mean?

  • $s-t$ graph slope = Velocity ($v$)
  • $v-t$ graph slope = Acceleration ($a$)
  • Curved $s-t$ line = Changing velocity (non-zero acceleration).
📊

What do CURVE AREAS mean?

  • Area under $v-t$ graph = Displacement ($\Delta s$)
  • Area under $a-t$ graph = Change in velocity ($\Delta v$)
  • Area below time axis = Negative contribution (reverse motion).

💡 Physics Kinematics Guide

Welcome to the enhanced Kinematics Studio Pro! This web application bridges real-world car motion with Displacement ($s$), Velocity ($v$), and Acceleration ($a$) graphs.

Key Sign Rules:

  • +vel & +acc: Moving forward, speeding up.
  • +vel & -acc: Moving forward, slowing down (braking).
  • -vel & +acc: Reversing, decelerating backward.
  • -vel & -acc: Reversing, speeding up backward.

Use the manual sliders or preset scenarios to experiment and observe live log updates!
